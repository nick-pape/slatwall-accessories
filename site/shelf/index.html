The 3MF carries the print settings this shelf was sized for: Prusa MK4/MK4S, 0.6 mm high-flow nozzle, PETG, 0.40 mm layers. The load rating assumes those settings. With other settings, use the STL and keep at least the same perimeters.

Part of slatwall-accessories. Rated loads come from a 2D finite-element model (creep 10 MPa, strength ÷ 2, buckling ÷ 3, tip sag ≤ depth/300). They're estimates, not a guarantee. Don't hang anything over people.
